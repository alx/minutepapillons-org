Frequently asked questions

I took a photo this weekend, I was charged but couldn’t retrieve the photos, what should I do?

Do not hesitate to contact me at contact@minutepapillons.org, I will see if I can retrieve them and send them to you, the old-fashioned way by post. Otherwise I will give you a refund.

How much does installing a booth in my venue cost?

Installing the photomaton booth is entirely free for you. I cover the design, the construction, the installation and the maintenance of the booth. In return, you receive a payment for hosting the booth in your space. This payment is calculated fairly according to the footfall and traffic of your venue.

Who handles the maintenance, changing the paper, dealing with problems and refunds?

Same thing: I take care of 100% of it myself, and customers find all the contact info in the booth in case of a problem. You do not have to deal with anything.

What is the lead time for setting up a booth?

From the moment we have decided to start the project, allow 1 month from the creation of the 3D model, through construction, to delivery. All my booths are one-of-a-kind pieces, made by hand.

What are the dimensions of the booths?

For a « classic » booth: 1.4m wide, 80cm deep and 2.2m tall including the sign.

For a photophone: 1m × 1m on the ground and 2.4m tall.

Can the photo strip design be modified?

We provide an initial model, but it is entirely possible for you to create your own design, applicable for the length of one evening, from a dedicated web interface.
