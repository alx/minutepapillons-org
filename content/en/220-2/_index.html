the little history

My adventure was born of a simple passion: capturing the moment, for real. It began in the middle of the desert with a simple cardboard box, and it continues today in my workshop with the most innovative photobooths.

It all started in 2013 at Burning Man. Armed with a cardboard box, photographic paper and chemicals, I was taking portraits of the extraordinary creatures of this festival. A rudimentary photobooth.

This first experience was followed by an Afghan camera box that accompanied me for several years to international festivals (Burning Man, Afrikaburn, Nowhere). A trace of this work can be seen here.

In 2017, the idea sprouted: to acquire a film photomaton. But these machines being unfindable or out of reach, only one solution imposed itself: to build one from scratch.

After several months of work, a first version was presented at the THSF festival, at Mixart Myrys. It is the first film booth produced in 30 years. It works… but could be improved!

Several years of intensive research and development in my living room followed.

In 2021, I left my job as a software engineer and founded Minute Papillons with Maritxu, with a mission: to bring film photography back into style. Our first device, the Carriole Photo, combines the optics of the Afghan Box with the developing process of the photomaton. We roam the streets, the markets, the bars, the festivals and even the nursing homes to share this magic.

In 2022, we installed our first film booth at Les Halles 500. Very quickly, the war in Ukraine caused the production of photomaton paper to stop at the sole factory, located in Russia. Reluctantly, we converted it to digital. To our dismay, but we discovered to our surprise that digital opens up new creative possibilities.

We then explored every facet of the photobooth:

At the end of 2022, a pocket film photomaton was created with the eye of Géraldine Terral. The idea being to start from the parts of an old 3D printer and from salvage: Le Selfimatik

In 2023, Maritxu left the adventure to devote herself to other desires and created the photo gallery « La Petite ».

At the end of 2023, we moved the workshop out of my living room into a real workspace. A new creation in collaboration with Alex Girard emerged: La Voix de son Maître, exploring the possibilities of AI.

In 2024 the first Photophone was created, opening up other perspectives than the head-on portrait. Two intimate spaces brought together in the public space: a telephone booth and a photo booth.

Today, in 2025, thanks to our digital booths, we close the loop. We can finally fund the R&D of a new film photomaton: the FlashMatik. More refined, more performant. Its first public outing at Faites de l’Image was a success, confirming that this idea, born in the desert, continues to bear fruit.
